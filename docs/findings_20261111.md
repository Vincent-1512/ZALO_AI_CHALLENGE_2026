## Dev score vs ASR quality (v1 merged model)
Exact ASR text: 69.9 (47 samples); WER 0.1-0.25: 44.8 (49); WER > 0.25: 33.3 (21). Typical ASR errors: proper names and foreign names, brand/drug names, spoken numbers, dialect words.

## Errors with perfect text (35 samples), hand-classified (a sample may fit two)
- Conditional requests and dependence on tool results ("if A then X else Y"): ~11
- Choosing the wrong ID from earlier tool output or history: ~7
- `sys.ask_missing_info` missed (4) or over-used (1): ~5
- Arithmetic / number copying (time shifts, amounts, percentages, phone digits): ~5
- Missing or mis-formatted arguments (`seat_class`, `days`, time formats): ~3
- Early stop with empty prediction: ~2
- Ambiguous labels (gold empty although the request has work to do): ~2

## Interpretation
- ASR costs ~13 points; the policy model costs ~32 more with perfect text. The ceiling for ASR-only work is ~67 on this model.
- Candidates sampled at temperature 0.7 mostly agree: errors are systematic. Fixes must come from data/training or code-side constraints, not decoding tricks.
- Gold-only SFT vs ASR-noise SFT: indistinguishable on dev (54.62 vs 53.46). The alt-ASR (PhoWhisper-large base) transcripts are ~25% of samples only in the 508-step noise stage, i.e. ~6% of v1's total training steps.
- Dev is from the same distribution as train (same apps, likely same speakers); public has unseen apps, so dev probably overestimates.
- The `large_ft` ASR memorised its training audio (model card): real WER on unseen speakers/apps is likely higher than the 9% dev WER.
